## What's new

- Light theme is now the dark scene with its lights and darks swapped (pale sky, dark moon), window tab included
- 8 colour palettes (Settings → Advanced → Palette), each with a dark and a light form
- Skybox: "My skyboxes" — save your own set under a name, rename it, apply it any time; previews come from one of its faces
- Skybox reset now also turns off hand-made skybox profiles, so the faces no longer light up again
- Skybox presets/templates and saved skyboxes may share a name
- FastFlags: "Reset all flags"; flag profiles have proper spacing
- Skybox fix is now automatic (no more switch in Settings): it is applied whenever a skybox is, and removed when you reset it
- ReCache: keeps a copy of Roblox's cache so clearing it doesn't mean downloading everything again
- Update notice at the top right when a new version is out; these notes show once after updating
- Luna's own tooltips, a Delete? button that goes back to × when you click elsewhere, no more panel jitter on Load / preset clicks, readable secondary text in both themes
